## What I do

- Analyze code for algorithmic complexity and performance anti-patterns
- Identify unnecessary rendering, allocations, and heavy synchronous work
- Find missing or ineffective caching opportunities
- Produce a prioritized optimization plan with estimated impact

## When to use me

Use this skill when reviewing performance before launch, investigating slow pages, reducing bundle/render cost, or planning optimization work.
